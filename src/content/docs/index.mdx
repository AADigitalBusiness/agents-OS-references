AI is no longer just models and prompts. Real AI products combine models, tools, retrieval, workflows, user interfaces, permissions, evaluations, and operational evidence.

This site helps intermediate learners understand that larger system.

## What You Will Learn

- How agent runtimes differ from workflow engines.
- Why retrieval and memory are related but not the same.
- Where governance, evaluation, and evidence fit in AI systems.
- How to compare open-source AI repositories without getting lost.
- Which patterns are useful for real products and which are still experimental.

## Editorial Standard

We prefer clear explanations, source-linked claims, practical tradeoffs, and reusable patterns over excitement or vague predictions.
