Prompting is only the entry point. Real AI systems need structure around the model.

A production AI system usually includes retrieval, tool access, workflow state, approvals, monitoring, and a way to inspect what happened.

## The Shift

Early AI experiments ask:

> What can the model say?

AI systems ask:

> What can the system do safely, repeatedly, and with evidence?

That shift changes the architecture.

## The Practical Stack

A useful AI system separates:

- The model that reasons or generates.
- The runtime that executes the agent loop.
- The workflow that controls process state.
- The knowledge system that retrieves context.
- The governance layer that limits authority.
- The evaluation layer that checks results.

Once you see those layers, frameworks become easier to compare.
