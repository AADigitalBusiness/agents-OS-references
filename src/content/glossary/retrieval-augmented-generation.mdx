---
title: Retrieval-Augmented Generation
definition: A pattern where an AI system searches external knowledge and gives the model retrieved context before generating an answer.
aliases:
  - RAG
related:
  - vector search
  - citations
  - knowledge base
status: active
---

Retrieval-augmented generation helps a model answer from a specific knowledge source instead of relying only on training data.

Good RAG systems pay attention to document preparation, chunking, ranking, citations, and freshness.
